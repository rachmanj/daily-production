# Training — Management

## Dashboard Executive

1. Buka **Dashboard** — 4 KPI cards: OB, Coal, SR, Fuel
2. Filter site dan tanggal di header
3. Chart tren 30 hari, per-PIT breakdown
4. Klik drill-down untuk detail PIT

## Plan vs Actual

1. **Plan → Monthly Plans** — input target OB/Coal per PIT
2. **Variance** — analisis selisih plan vs actual + kontribusi hujan/licin

## Reports

1. **Reports → Daily** — PDF/Excel laporan harian (template ARKA/ENG/IV/12.01)
2. **Custom** — filter rentang tanggal, PIT, export PDF/Excel

## Procurement

1. **Procurement** — kartu Budget, GRPO, NPI, CAPEX dari ARK-GS
2. Indikator "last synced" per kartu
